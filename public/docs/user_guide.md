# MicsBook user guide

MicsBook is a private ebook library that runs in your browser and works on your
phone, tablet, and computer. Sign in, then read, listen to, and organize your
books anywhere — even offline once installed as an app.

## Signing in

- Use the email account your administrator created, or **Google** sign-in when it
  is enabled for your library.
- The first account created on a fresh library automatically becomes the
  **administrator**.

## Finding books

The top navigation gives you several ways to browse:

- **Books** — the full library, newest first. Use the view buttons to switch
  between the card grid and a compact list, and pick a sort order.
- **Authors** and **Series** — browse by name; each page lists the matching books.
- **Search** — search by title, author, or series.
- **Random** — a surprise pick from your library.

Every book page shows the cover, description, author, series, and publish date,
with buttons to **Read** and **Download**.

## Reading

- **EPUB** books open in the built-in reader with a table of contents, page
  numbers, and a location bar.
- **PDF** books open in the built-in PDF viewer.
- Your place is remembered, so you can close the book and resume where you left
  off later.

While reading, the **reader tools** let you:

- **Bookmark** your current spot.
- Write a **note** about any paragraph, with a quote from the book.
- Jump back to your bookmarks and notes.

Bookmarks and notes are private to your account.

## Listen (read aloud)

Press the round play button at the bottom of the EPUB reader to have the book
read aloud. Use the options button next to it to:

- Turn on **Stop after play time** and choose a number of minutes — reading aloud
  will stop after that amount of listening time.
- Turn on **Keep screen on while reading** so the screen stays awake while the
  highlighted paragraph is spoken.

Playback continues while you switch apps or lock the screen on supported
browsers; use the media controls in the notification shade or lock screen to
pause, resume, or stop.

## My Library

**My Library** is your private space for everything personal:

- **Recent** — the books you have read most recently.
- **Lists** — create named lists such as *Summer reads* and add books to them
  from any book page.
- **Tags** — label books however you like and browse by tag.

Everything in My Library is visible only to you.

## Sharing a book

On any book page, the **Share this book** section (when your account is allowed
to share links) shows:

- A **Share…** button that uses your device's native share sheet.
- Quick links for **WhatsApp**, **Telegram**, **Facebook**, and **X** that open a
  pre-filled share message.
- A **Copy link** button to copy the book's address to your clipboard.

The person who receives the link can open the book if they have access to this
library.

## Downloading

Use the **Download** button on any book page to save the file to your device in
its original EPUB or PDF format.

## Install the app

MicsBook is a progressive web app, so you can install it like a native app:

- **Android / Chrome**: choose *Add to Home screen* or the install icon in the
  address bar.
- **iOS / Safari**: tap the *Share* button, then *Add to Home Screen*.

Once installed, the app opens full-screen, starts faster, and books you have
opened stay available when you are offline.

## Roles

Libraries use role-based access:

- **Reader** — browse, read, download, and use personal features.
- **Manager** — everything a reader can do, plus library management in
  **Admin → Library** (upload books, rescan, and recover-delete).
- **Admin** — full control, including users and settings in **Admin**.

Admins can manage accounts under **Admin → Users**: change roles and activity,
see each user's last login and last IP address, the books they have read, and
reset a password if needed.

## Need help?

Contact your library administrator if you cannot sign in, if a book will not
open, or if a feature is missing.
